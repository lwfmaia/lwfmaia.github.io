Privacy Policy – Hermes Personal

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Hermes Personal is a private application used only by its owner, Leonardo Maia. It is not available to other users.

Data accessed

  • Google Calendar (calendar.app.created): only calendars and events the app itself created.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): only files the app created or that the owner explicitly opened with it.

Use and storage

Data is used solely to perform tasks the owner requests. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
